# Skyline and metropolis scenery guidance

This file records visual constraints from recent skyline work. A former attempt to add dense low-rise housing behind the metropolis looked horrifying in daytime and was rejected by the user. Do not add that background housing again unless the user explicitly requests it. Keep the accepted skyline and its existing depth intact.

## Metropolis boundaries

- Add no neighborhood, house row, building strip, or other new housing behind the existing metropolis. Keep the existing skyline towers, distant horizon, hill and woodland trees, paths, and lake reflection.
- Do not add separate tiny foreground trees around the metropolis. They were explicitly removed. This does not remove or change the established trees on the rolling hills or in the woodland.
- Do not fill the former housing gap by raising individual structures or extending the skyline. The horizon and existing skyline composition remain as they are.
- Rooftop gardens and patios may appear only on eligible shorter towers. Exclude the tallest tier and every lightning-rod tower from both types. Read the tower's shared deterministic `hasLightningRod` value for both selection and painting.
- Each eligible garden and patio string starts with an independent one-in-twelve chance of being lit, so all-on and all-off skylines remain possible. Keep each roof's sampled state stable between paints and while temporarily ineligible after a resize. On the shared UTC clock, opportunities stay spaced one minute apart; at a nighttime opportunity, select one eligible garden or patio and independently resample its state with the same one-in-twelve chance. The sampled state may remain unchanged. Patio strings use the same retained per-roof state as garden fixtures. Hide illuminated bulbs and strings whenever the Sun is at or above the horizon, even if that roof's retained sample is lit. Normalized window samples use staggered seeded phases on the shared 15-second UTC grid. These doubled opportunity rates preserve brightness, population and independent odds without a catch-up burst.

## Clocktower visitors

- Peter Pan, Wendy, John and Michael form one rare night-only visit: one 2% start roll per UTC nighttime minute, followed by 50 UTC seconds for the whole arrival, rest and departure. Start the next minute after expiry; never replay missed opportunities or stack visits. The separate `clocktower-visit` spawn rate controls only the chance.
- Keep the accepted clocktower size and location. Share the actual left/apex/right roof triangle with the landing geometry. Two children stand on each sloped edge, and both feet of each landed silhouette touch that edge. Stagger arrival, landing and departure with continuous eased paths; preserve a quiet common pause.
- Paint small muted silhouettes in the existing dynamic scene and reflection. Daylight cancels immediately. Hidden tabs stop painting and reconstruct the current UTC visit on return. Reduced motion shows fixed landed silhouettes with gentle opacity changes on the existing one-second ambient timer, without moving flight, limbs or posture. Resizing recomputes contact against the newly painted roof.

## Rooftop patios and motion

- Some eligible rooftop gardens may be replaced by patios; retain planted gardens on other eligible roofs. Party eligibility is structural and independent of patio/garden assignment: every shorter usable roof below the tallest tier and without a lightning rod can host an event.
- A permanent patio has two small posts, one gently sagging string, and warm fairy-light dots. Colored party fixtures are temporary; do not leave party bulbs or beams running on every patio.
- Schedule one rooftop party at most for the entire city. Make one independent 2% start roll after each 30 seconds of UTC nighttime, then keep the event active for 24 UTC seconds. After it ends, reserve the next 30-second opportunity after a selected visit. This yields about one party per 25 UTC night minutes on average. Select uniformly from every structurally eligible short, non-rod roof, including gardens and plain terraces; permanent patio assignment must not limit reachability. The runtime uses keyed scenery randomness, while tests inject deterministic draws to check reachability for each roof. Keep this schedule independent from festival/firework cooldowns and never animate missed rolls as a replay backlog.
- At night, the selected event's filled spotlight cones rise from that roof and scan smoothly up and down over a short distance. Each cone starts narrow at its fixture, widens softly outward, and fades naturally; thin laser-like center-lines or bright far-tip dots do not satisfy this request. Keep the beams close to their own roof. At most one roof may have colored party lights and beams at a time. Daylight hides the event; a resize maps the selected normalized roof to the nearest eligible visible roof.
- Opaque foreground towers and the civic clock tower must hide any part of a party fixture or cone behind their silhouettes. Treat overlapping buildings as a true union mask; one even-odd path can reopen overlaps and let beams show through a facade. Keep the deterministic 390×844 roof-19/clock-tower overlap in the occlusion regression.
- Read the UTC snapshot once per rendered scene tick. Hidden tabs pause rendering, then reconstruct the current UTC snapshot without replay. Reduced motion still permits event selection and its normal UTC expiry, but holds the beams at one static pose. Paint party lights through the dynamic scene/reflection path, not in the cached background.
- Daylight patio hardware stays subtle and illuminated bulbs and strings are hidden; at night, independently sampled string lights and the rare single-roof party remain readable without competing with the skyline.

Do not bring back the rejected enlarged shaded wings, colored fuselage, cockpit dots, or panel detail without a new explicit request.

Keep the preview date, season, weather, and random seed consistent when comparing renders; use the same green summer foliage for before/after evidence unless the task specifically changes season behavior.

For candidate-art review, use a fresh isolated preview origin and verify the served candidate asset bytes/build before judging an unchanged render; an older service worker can keep stale scene code active and make a new draft appear unchanged. Treat this as a candidate visual check only. Final release verification must separately exercise the real service worker, deployed assets, and an actual offline reload.

Review at least desktop (about 1280×720) and phone (about 390×844) in daytime and night, plus short landscape when vertical placement or motion changes. Confirm the rejected housing and city-edge microtrees are absent, established hill/woodland trees and skyline reflection remain, eligible gardens/patios/events avoid tall or rod-equipped towers, and warm patio strings stay attached to their own roofs. Confirm that no more than one temporary party is active, that party lights are absent between events, and that every structurally eligible roof can be selected. For moving beams, compare two night frames of the same selected event at different times and verify a visible vertical sweep in normal motion plus a fixed reduced-motion pose and expiry. A close-up helps diagnose roof detail but cannot replace full-scene acceptance.

## Rain and fireworks

- Rain forms a dense curtain across phone, tablet, and short-landscape scenes. Falling streaks paint once over the composed scene and lake; do not mirror them into upward streaks. Clouds, snow, and distant lightning retain their existing reflections. Reduced motion holds a deterministic static rain pose.
- Random fireworks run for one sampled duration between 60 and 300 seconds, with repeating bursts and a complete final fade. Their particles stay behind the real opaque skyline and clock tower in both the scene and its reflection. Festival-barge shells stay in the foreground and retain their deck launch positions, scale, and crossing duration.

The shared-scene request supersedes earlier active-time pause rules: all event selection now uses `landscape-timeline.js` and UTC age, with still poses for reduced motion. Keep the accepted responsive geometry; selected normalized roof events adapt to the nearest structurally eligible visible roof. The scenery has a shared immutable default seed, distinct from saved scanner Chance seeds, and never writes a board on a scene tick.
